## Evidence policy
- Release-specific additions and Beta status come from official release notes and Beta announcements.
- Ordinary long-standing tool workflows are corroborated by Photoshop desktop documentation and organized for education; not every individual control was exercised in Photoshop.
- Tool shortcut letters and common Windows commands are conservative reference values; the installed Edit > Keyboard Shortcuts dialog remains authoritative after customizations.
- Labels and offerings of cloud/AI models, credits and subscription entitlements can change without desktop version change.
- GPU hardware requirements are official; virtualization recommendations are practical diagnostic guidance, not official Adobe VM certification.
- Shortcut, tool/filter and operating-system support claims not independently simulated inside Photoshop are marked as requiring live-application verification in AUDIT.md.
